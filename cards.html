Cards & stickers

One card goes inside each Pokeball. All cards look the same, so the surprise stays hidden. The label under each card (cut it off) tells you which creature it opens. Print at 100% / actual size.

Printer settings: double-sided, flip on long edge, 100% / actual size. Each front page is followed by its back page. Do one test sheet first and hold it up to a light to check the fronts and backs line up.

NP stickers

The same sticker goes flat on the floor of every Pokeball. It's what the camera looks for. Use one size for all your balls: a bigger sticker means a bigger Pokémon. Matte sticker paper works best (gloss reflects light).
