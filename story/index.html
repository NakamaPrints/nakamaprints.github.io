03 · The magic bit

Scan. Release. Pop!

Four taps from a closed ball to a Pokémon in your Pokédex. These are real screens from the app.

1

Open it up, scan the card

Pop the ball open. The NP coin is waiting inside. Scan the card's QR with your normal phone camera. Nothing to install, it opens in your browser.

Tap the card to flip it. On a computer? Scan this one with your phone to try the demo.

2

Tap RELEASE! and point at the coin

A white flash, a burst of sparks, and out it hops, standing right on the coin with a little cry. Walk around it and it turns to follow you.

3

The Pokédex speaks

After a few seconds the red Pokédex slides up and reads the entry out loud. In that voice, the one from the anime. Try it:

No.004

CHARMANDER

Lizard Pokémon

Tap ▶ Hear it (sound on).

4

Add it to your Pokédex

One tap on ADD TO MY POKÉDEX and it's yours. Then go find the next one.

    1. Type in a friend's trainer code. Their Pokédex opens up.
    2. Pick one of theirs and choose what you'll give for it.
    3. Send the offer. They accept or decline. Accept, and the swap happens straight away.
