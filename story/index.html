07 · Help

Ask the Professor

Is this an official Pokémon product?

No. It's a fan project I make for friends and family. It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Creatures or GAME FREAK.

Can I buy one?

The Pokémon version isn't for sale: it's a gift for my friends. If you'd want something like it, start a save file below and I'll let you know if it becomes real.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Scan the card with your normal camera and it opens in your browser. That's it.

Which phones does it work on?

Any recent iPhone or Android phone with a camera, in Safari or Chrome. Good light helps the camera find the coin.
