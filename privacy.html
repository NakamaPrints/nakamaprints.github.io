Privacy

Nakama Pokédex · last updated 27 October 2026

Nakama Pokédex is a small, free, personal project by Nakama Prints: 3D-printed Pokeballs given to friends and family, with a web page that shows the Pokémon inside in augmented reality and keeps a Pokédex of the ones you collect. It is not a commercial service and has no ads.

Your camera

The page uses your camera only to find the NP coin inside the Pokeball. The video stays on your phone. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.

What we store

  • A trainer account. The first time you add a Pokémon, your phone gets an anonymous guest account. It has a trainer code (like NP-4821) and a trainer name you can choose.
  • Your Pokédex. Which Pokémon you have, which card each one came from, and when you added it.
  • If you save your Pokédex with Google or an email link, we store your email address (and, with Google, the name on your Google account) so you can sign in again on another phone. We only ask Google for your name and email, nothing else from your Google account.
  • On your phone the page keeps your sign-in and a few settings (like sound on or off) in the browser's local storage.
  • If you join the "Want one?" list on the story page, we store the email or Instagram handle you type, plus your name, who it would be for and your note if you add them. We use it only to tell you if Nakama creatures become real. The list is private: the page can add you and show how many people have joined, nothing more.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking or analytics, no selling or sharing your data.
  • Other trainers only see your trainer name, trainer code and Pokémon, and only when you trade with them.

Where it's kept

The data is stored with Supabase, the database service this project runs on. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

Want your Pokédex and account deleted? Send a message to @nakamaprints_in on Instagram with your trainer code, and it will be removed. To leave the "Want one?" list, send the email or handle you signed up with.
