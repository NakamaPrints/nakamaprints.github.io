The demo

Your screen is the Pokeball

Grab your phone. Scan the card, then point your camera at the coin, and see who pops out.

On a phone? This demo needs two screens: open nakamaprints.github.io/try on a computer or tablet, then scan it with your phone.

Watch one pop out here No camera needed

1 · Scan the card

Open your phone's camera and scan the QR. Then tap RELEASE!

In real life, this card comes with your Pokeball.

2 · Point at the coin

Hold your phone about 20–30 cm from the screen and aim at this coin.

In real life, this coin sits inside your Pokeball.

3 · Watch it pop out

A Pokémon jumps out of the coin, right on your screen, and the Pokédex reads its entry out loud. Every ball is a surprise, so scan again for another one.

Tips: turn your screen brightness up, and sound on for the Pokédex voice.

No phone handy? Watch it without a camera ▸
